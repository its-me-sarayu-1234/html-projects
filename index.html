Fun Facts About the Ocean

  • The ocean covers more than 70% of the Earth's surface.
  • There are over 230,000 known species of marine life.
  • The deepest part of the ocean is the Mariana Trench, which is about 36,000 feet deep.

Dolphins

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals known for their playful behavior and acrobatics. They communicate using a variety of sounds and are often seen swimming in pods.
