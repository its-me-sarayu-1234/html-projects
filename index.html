Building a Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A healthy plate gives our body the energy it needs to study, play, think, and grow. It should include colourful fruits, fresh vegetables, grains, and protein-rich food.

Learn more about healthy eating here: MyPlate Nutrition Guide

Smart Snack Choices

Snacks can be healthy when we choose foods that give energy instead of only sugar. Fruits, nuts, sprouts, and yoghurt are better choices than chips and sugary drinks.

Explore more nutrition facts here: WHO Healthy Diet Guide

Nutrition Tips for Students

Drink Enough Water

Water helps the body stay active, supports digestion, and keeps the brain focused. Students should carry a water bottle and drink water throughout the day.

Eat Colourful Food

Different colours in fruits and vegetables give different nutrients. A colourful plate often means a healthier plate.

Read more about healthy food choices here: Nutrition.gov

Quick Nutrition Note

Try this simple rule: fill half your plate with fruits and vegetables, one quarter with grains, and one quarter with protein.

the CDC Healthy Eating Guide provides tips and resources for maintaining a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle.

Facts on Healthy Eating, lifestyle and exercise

  • Healthy eating is essential for maintaining overall health and well-being.
  • Incorporating a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins into your diet can help prevent chronic diseases and promote longevity.
  • Regular physical activity, combined with a balanced diet, is crucial for maintaining a healthy weight and reducing the risk of obesity-related conditions.
  • Staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water is important for digestion, circulation, and temperature regulation.
  • Limiting the intake of processed foods, added sugars, and saturated fats can contribute to better heart health and overall wellness.
  • Mindful eating practices, such as paying attention to hunger and fullness cues, can help prevent overeating and promote a healthier relationship with food.
  • Consulting with a registered dietitian or nutritionist can provide personalized guidance and support for achieving specific health and nutrition goals.
  • Incorporating regular exercise, such as walking, jogging, or strength training, can enhance physical fitness, improve mood, and boost energy levels.
  • Getting adequate sleep is essential for overall health, as it supports cognitive function, immune system health, and emotional well-being.
  • Maintaining a balanced lifestyle that includes healthy eating, regular exercise, and stress management can lead to improved quality of life and overall well-being.
  • Engaging in social activities and building strong relationships can positively impact mental health and contribute to a sense of community and support.
  • Practicing stress-reducing techniques, such as meditation, deep breathing exercises, or yoga, can help manage stress levels and promote relaxation.
  • Regular health check-ups and screenings can help detect potential health issues early and allow for timely intervention and treatment.
